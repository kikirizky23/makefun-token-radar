Token radar: the pre-token board

Every protocol that records no token, ranked by the value its token might carry at launch — if it priced like the live-token protocols in its own category.

How this board is built

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier × chain multiplier

Peer rule: peers are protocols with a market cap above zero; each peer’s ratio is market cap ÷ TVL, winsorized to [0.01, 1.50]. A category’s multiple is the median peer ratio when the category has at least 3 peers, otherwise the global median of 0.2138.

“Records no token” means DefiLlama records neither a CoinGecko id nor a ticker for the protocol. Implied value is a model, not a promise: an estimate of what a token could be worth, not a price, a launch date, or a guarantee that a token ever appears.

Data as of 2026-11-14, from DefiLlama. Model radar-3. Every number on this page is recomputed in the open — see app.js.
